THE FULL CATALOG
// 6 CHANNELS · ~25 MESSAGE TYPES · ALL USER-CONTROLLED
ﷺ PRAYER
الصلاة
Daily azan notifications, tied to the user's city + calculation method. 5 prayers × up to 2 nudges each.
EXAMPLES
"Fajr in 10 minutes · 5:18 AM"
"Time for Dhuhr · 13:08"
"Maghrib · Sun sets at 6:52 PM"
"You haven't marked Asr as prayed"
"Jummah in 1 hour — leave early"
◆ GYM
صالة الرياضة
Evening reminders, split-day hints, streak protection. Fires only on days you haven't logged.
EXAMPLES
"Push day scheduled · chest / shoulders / tris"
"You haven't logged the gym today"
"2 hours till midnight — save the streak"
"You hit your 16-day target for October 🔥"
◈ MEALS
وجبات
Calorie + macro nudges at meal times, tied to the target you set in onboarding.
EXAMPLES
"1,240 / 2,400 kcal left · log lunch"
"Protein: 45/180g — you're short today"
"Iftar in 20 min · plan your break"
"Hit your daily target — nicely done"
◈ JOURNAL
اليومي
Evening nudge to close out the day — workout, meals, water, mood, notes.
EXAMPLES
"Close out your day · 4 fields left"
"Log your mood before bed"
"Hydration: 3/8 glasses today"
◉ SQUAD
الإخوة / الأخوات
Brothers'/Sisters' activity + manager broadcasts. Gender-filtered so no mixed feeds.
EXAMPLES
"Mustafa just hit the gym · 15 sessions"
"Zaid beat his squat PR · 140kg"
"Broadcast from the Manager"
"You're #3 on this week's leaderboard"
☾ MOTIVATION
تذكير
Streak milestones, hadith quotes, duas for strength. Low frequency — once or twice a week.
EXAMPLES
"Your streak: 27 days. Keep it unbroken."
"The strong believer is better than the weak one — Muslim"
"30 days without missing Fajr. Alhamdulillah."
